
# Overview of Organizations that Use the AccelRR8 Brand

In an [earlier story](../My_AccelRR8_Brand/Introducing_AccelRR8.md) I explained that Businesses and Non-Profit Organizations under the AccelRR8 brand are an extension of my philosophy and capabilities in pursuit of my interests.

The **AccelRR8 Consortium Non-Profit** is a 501(c)3 non-profit organization through which I and other participants help Technology Services industry leaders collaborate to help humanity by speeding up the industry’s (1) shift from project-to-product and (2) use of exponential accelerators.

**AccelRR8 LLC** is a for-profit company through which I and my associates provide fee-based services to help leaders accelerate exponential success for themselves, for their customers and colleagues, and for humanity.

AccelRR8 Consortium Non-Profit and AccelRR8 LLC are unaffiliated with one another. Both are licensed to use the AccelRR8 brand. AccelRR8 LLC is a patron of the AccelRR8 Consortium NonProfit.

I am the founder and President of the AccelRR8 Consortium Non-Profit organization. My wife Sherry Reba is the sole owner of the AccelRR8 LLC business. AccelRR8 LLC may participate in government programs available to woman-owned businesses in the future.

After a period of learning, we may consider merging these two organizations in a model perhaps similar to OpenAI, which is a non-profit organization with a for-profit business as a wholly owned subsidiary.
