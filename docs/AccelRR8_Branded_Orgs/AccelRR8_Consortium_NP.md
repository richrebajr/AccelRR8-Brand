
A few years ago I said to my colleagues on LinkedIn that I wanted to share my learnings to help professionals across the tech services industry actualize their full collective potential.

I asked them to point me to a community or forum focused on doing this, and I said if no such community exists then I will create one.

I couldn’t find such a community so I created the AccelRR8 Consortium.  
In a [previous story](./AccelRR8_Orgs_Overview.md) I explained that AccelRR8 Consortium NP is the non-profit organization under the AccelRR8 brand.

The AccelRR8 Consortium NonProfit has evolved considerably since its formation late in 2023.  To learn more, visit its website at https://www.accelrr8.org.
