
# Calling All Exponential Jedi !

Among many great ideas I came across while reading [**The Brand Mapping Strategy** by Karen Tiber Leland](https://sterlingmarketinggroup.com/the-brand-mapping-strategy/), one that stood out was telling a “Signature Story” about yourself that people find engaging. Karen goes on to identify five of the most common story archetypes. As I reflected on my life, I realized my Signature Story involves all five!

**1. The Wizard of Oz — A transformative journey in the 1990's** 
Since I was young, I’ve enjoyed dreaming of a better future, and then innovating and leading the charge to get there. At a top technical college and in the “Emerald City” (Army ROTC and officer corps), I learned how to innovate with technology, and how to lead in the technology industry.

**2. Cinderella — From humble beginnings, exponential success in the 2000's**
I achieved exponential success when I grew a global Technology Consulting practice from scratch to $100M+ revenues. I wanted to try to replicate that success across an entire portfolio of Fortune 500 products and services.

**3. Lord of the Rings — A noble quest fraught with peril in the 2010's**
But I found my success was a LOT harder to replicate across a Fortune 500 portfolio than I expected. So, I sailed my ship through a hurricane (the CSC and HP-E merger) and uncovered the secret of **_Exponential Accelerators_**.

**4. A Christmas Carol — A jaded soul gets a new outlook in the early 2020's**
After navigating one of the biggest tech mergers in history took its toll on my body and spirit, I took time to rejuvenate my family, health, and home, and rekindled my desire to help humanity and live life joyfully.

**5. Star Wars — A Jedi battles the dark side in the 21st century**
Now I want to share my learnings to help all Jedi in the technology services industry Use the Force (AI-enabled **_[Exponential Accelerators](./Exponential_Accelerators.md)_**) for good!

Together we have the potential to help the world’s leaders generate increasingly better products, services, financials, and inspiration, and to help people everywhere live increasingly better lives.

Let’s join Forces!
