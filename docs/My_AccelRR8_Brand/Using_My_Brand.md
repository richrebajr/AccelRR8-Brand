
# How I'll Use my Brand to Help in Tech Services

The Technology Services industry, in the free world, is a very large group of people who broadly share [my philosophy](../Personal_Reflections/My_Big_Questions.md), [talents, and interests](./My_Talents_and_Interests.md). The industry consists of businesses and organizations who provide and/or depend on Technology Services.

### Helping the Technology Services Industry be Exponentially Better

I believe that this industry has the potential to have a greater impact, by orders of magnitude, than it currently does, and that this impact can improve exponentially. I also believe that I can maximize my own impact, and by extension AccelRR8’s impact, by acting as a force multiplier that helps the Technology Services industry maximize its benefit to humanity.

### Accelerating the Project-to-Product Paradigm Shift

Businesses and organizations that are impactful in the present, and that have the potential to be exponentially more impactful in the future, understand how to improve the products they provide and depend on rapidly and continuously. They have made a paradigm shift broadly known as “project to product”.

Ironically, while businesses and organizations that produce software or are highly dependent on software as their primary means of value creation have an easier time making this paradigm shift, the Technology Services industry, which is more dependent today on people than software, is struggling to make the shift.

[My entire career](../Personal_Reflections/My_Signature_Story.md) has been about struggling to help the Technology Services industry make the project-to-product paradigm shift as a product leader or product portfolio leader, and I have learned many lessons that I believe can help the Technology Services industry accelerate progress.

I believe I and AccelRR8 will have the most influence and therefore impact if I “lead by example” in one or more Technology Services organizations (e.g., as a fractional CPO/CTO/CIO executive and/or consultant) and by leading a Technology Services industry consortium focused on accelerating the project-to-product paradigm shift and generating exponentially better products, services, financials, and inspiration in the Technology Services industry.
