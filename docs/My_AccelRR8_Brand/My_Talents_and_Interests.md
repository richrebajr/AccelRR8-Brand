
# How my Brand Reflects my Talents and Interests

In accordance with [my philosophy](../Personal_Reflections/My_Big_Questions.md) and my [AccelRR8 brand](./Introducing_AccelRR8.md)), I seek opportunities to use my chief talents and pursue my main interests to maximize my impact.

In my experience, people can accomplish much more when they work together than when they work individually, and so I seek opportunities to work with others who share my philosophy, talents, and interests.

### Providing Advice About Products and Technology

My chief talent, and by extension AccelRR8’s core competency, is advising large groups of leaders and collaborators on how best to use technology to generate exponentially better products, services, financials, and inspiration, accelerating exponential success for themselves _AND_ accelerating human progress.

My advice on how best to use technology, based on 30+ years of experience

1. seeks to achieve exponential **ENDS** whenever feasible, which are future outcomes that are orders of magnitude greater than current outcomes, and that are improving exponentially.
2. via technology **MEANS,** specifically [**AI-Enabled Exponential Accelerators**](../Personal_Reflections/Exponential_Accelerators.md)) that harness and exponentially improve **Information Technologies** used to generate exponentially better **products, services, and systems** across all **stages of development** in all **levels of maturity**
3. used in enabling **WAYS,** including all **providers and users** of technology in all **value streams** in all **industries,** using approaches that **maximize success** and **prepare for the future**

I’ll elaborate further on these concepts in subsequent stories.

### Preparing People for the Future

My main interest is helping people prepare for the exciting and terrifying future we all face. As I look to the future, I see that advances in artificial intelligence and machine learning will enable machines to do more and more work that people used to do. I want to prepare people to be able to perform meaningful work and earn a living for as long as they need to as the use of AI and ML increases.

In my experience, techniques such as the [Scaled Agile Framework](https://scaledagileframework.com/) provide a methodology that can allow people at all skill levels to provide meaningful work in the foreseeable future, in a way that minimizes their suffering and increases joy; I want to help large groups of people use these sort of techniques to this end.

In parallel I want to prepare people — myself and my family included — for a future when AI and ML can do everything people can do, as well as other no-longer-unthinkable circumstances such as collective consciousness (e.g., the “singularity”) and extreme longevity; so that these new circumstances decrease our suffering and increase joy as each emerges.
